# Data and hardware

The hardware (FMG band, EMG sleeve, Arduino rig) and every recording made with it belong to the
club and its volunteers. **This repository contains none of it**: no recordings, no logs, no
screenshots of real sessions.

- `test/fixtures/synth_*` are synthetic, made by `tools/make-synthetic-fixtures.mjs`.
- The digital twin (`src/sim/`) makes synthetic signals for tests and the `?demo` mode. Its parameters here are rounded stand-ins, not values fitted on club logs.
- Public datasets (e.g. Ninapro DB5, CC-BY-ND) are converted locally and never redistributed.

Synthetic data is for testing and demos, never evidence of accuracy. Not a medical device.
